## What we commit to

- **Issues and pull requests:** a first reply within 7 days.
- **Security reports:** handled as described in [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md).
- **Releases:** a tagged release with notes at least once a quarter while there are changes on `main`.
- **Hermes upstream:** we update the `hermes-agent` submodule at least once a quarter, and send fixes for
  Hermes upstream before we carry a local patch.

If we can no longer keep these commitments, we will say so here and in the README, and either find a new
maintainer or archive the repository. We will not leave it quietly unmaintained.

## Promises about the license

The maintainers are accountable for these promises.

1. Everything released here under MIT stays under MIT. We will not re-license released code to a
   source-available or proprietary license.
2. Features released here will not move into a paid or closed edition.
3. We will not add artificial limits on users, sources, channels or message volume to this repository.
4. Tests for open features ship with the features.
5. If we ever change what we publish here, we will announce it in an issue at least 30 days before it
   takes effect, with the reasons.

MUST Company may sell setup, customization and support services for Open Agent. Those services do not
change what is published here.

## Decisions

Either maintainer decides on day-to-day changes. Changes to the promises above or to the license need
both maintainers to agree and are announced as described in promise 5.
